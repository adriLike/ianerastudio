Where to start

  1. 01
    Unzip the whole folder before you open anything. If you open the .als from inside the zip, Ableton won't find the samples and some tracks will play silent. It's the most common issue and there's nothing more to it.
  2. 02
    Open the project and hit play before you touch anything. Listen through once. Knowing where the arrangement goes before you start pulling it apart saves you half an hour.
  3. 03
    Start at the end, not the beginning. Go to the drop, solo the lead group and switch devices off one at a time. Hearing what's missing teaches you more than reading what's there.
  4. 04
    Look at the automation before the presets. That's where half the movement of the track lives, and it's what almost nobody copies when they try to work a sound out by ear.
